# First-person weapon presentation

Revolvers and knives use the same camera-only R6 renderer. They appear only at full zoom (0.55-stud entry, 0.6-stud exit tolerance). The renderer copies the equipped skin, classic shirt and avatar arms, with thinner arms and restrained sway/bob. Revolver aiming moves the hold left and down; recoil and reload motion remain smooth. Knives show one hand, a progressive charge pose, a prepared forward stab with a brief extended hold and throw release. The held knife disappears during its existing 0.15–0.65-second return window.

No skin particles, beams, trails, lights or legacy effects are cloned. Original weapon effects are locally suppressed while the model is active and restored when leaving first person. Original decals and textures are explicitly masked too: a hidden parent part alone can leave its face image visible. The camera skin retains its authored opacity. Unsupported decal modifiers use a local Transparency fallback with separate authored values, so hiding the real texture never hides its camera copy.

First-person revolver firing shows a short muzzle flash, sparks and a visible bullet streak from the configured cosmetic muzzle toward the shot destination, alongside audio, recoil and confirmed impact feedback. Two reusable emitters use Rate=0 and Emit() bursts: two flash particles lasting 55 ms and four sparks lasting 80 ms. Three cached sound voices allow overlapping shots. Two cached streak slots reuse their beams, trails and glints. The prepared muzzle/streak path creates no new Instances and scans no descendants during ordinary firing. Confirmed wall/floor hits lazily create reusable bullet-mark slots until their separate 24-mark cap. Streaks share the existing bounded tracer update, connected only while shots are active. Decorative skin effects stay suppressed; first-person smoke and duplicate world-muzzle flashes are omitted. Other players keep their normal world effects. Knife throws retain the visible blade and audio without added first-person trails or impact bursts.

The camera smoothly lowers 0.7 studs when crouching and 1 stud when sliding. Movement animation publishes its actual posture, so holding crouch in the air or after death does not retain the drop. Camera rotation stays with the native camera. Both camera position and Focus move down equally to preserve the zoom distance; hands and weapons follow the lowered camera. Body and shoulder animation add bounded local posture movement.

## Code and configuration

| Module | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| `WeaponViewmodel` | Independent manager per weapon: full-zoom gating, retries, local visibility ownership and cleanup |
| `WeaponViewmodelGeometry` | Safe shallow copies, original-to-copy appearance mapping, cached multipart placement and physics guards |
| `WeaponViewmodelEffects` | Local suppression/restoration of original skin effects; creates no effects |
| `WeaponViewmodelVisibility` | Cached original/copy decal and texture masks with authored-opacity restoration |
| `RevolverEffects.SetFirstPerson` | Prepares and reuses camera muzzle emitters, sound voices and streak slots |
| `RevolverViewmodel` / `KnifeViewmodel` | Weapon adapters for the shared renderer |
| `RevolverViewmodelMath` / `KnifeViewmodelMotion` | Smooth camera-local motion and knife charge/attack poses |
| `FirstPersonPostureCamera` | Applies the movement camera height after native camera and ADS updates |
| `FirstPersonToggle` | Saved ToogleCam input, native first-person lock and one-update zoom restoration |
| `RevolverToolPhysics` | Keeps the real held weapon noncolliding and massless on server and client |

The old `RevolverViewmodelGeometry` and `RevolverViewmodelEffects` names remain compatibility wrappers around the shared modules.

Tune revolvers in `RevolverConfig.Default.FirstPerson`, with per-skin `Overrides[skinName].FirstPerson`. `ShotEnabled`, `ShotFlashSize`, `ShotFlashLifetimeMs`, `ShotSparkCount`, `ShotSparkLifetimeMs`, `ShotSoundVoices` and `ShotTracerSlots` control firing feedback with bounded configuration validation. `Effects.TracerEnabled` and the existing tracer color/width/travel settings also apply in first person. Tune the knife in `KnifeConfig.FirstPerson`, including `ArmPosition`, `ChargeOffset`, `ChargeRotationDegrees`, `StabOffset`, `ThrowOffset` and their response speeds. Its hand now sits farther right: the arm center X is 1.02 studs with a further 0.08-stud charge offset. Its full-charge offset rises 1.05 studs in camera space with a 45-degree upward rotation. The wind-up smoothly blends into a fixed camera-local base, so switching the real character animation from Charge to ChargeHold cannot drop the hand. This pose remains raised for as long as the actual charge input remains held, then releases on throw/cancel; unequip clears its model and motion state. Native crouch/slide camera height still moves the view and hands together. Both weapons' arms default to 75% width/depth. Length and authored RightGrip remain intact. `SupportArmEnabled=false` hides the knife's left support arm.

Tune camera height independently in `FirstPersonCameraConfig`: `CrouchDrop`, `SlideDrop`, `FollowSpeed`, `EnterDistance` and `ExitDistance`. Camera and weapon zoom thresholds use the same defaults. Restart Play after changing module configuration.

The existing settings rows named `Reload` and `ToogleCam` use `KeybindValidation` defaults R and Q. Both are keyboard bindings and use the existing validated ChangeKeybind remote, profile autosave/session save, join migration and live UI updates. Reload input and its HUD hint follow the saved value immediately; gamepad and touch reload controls retain their existing behavior. Older custom bindings keep priority during migration, with an unused fallback if Q was already assigned. A one-time join migration moves the former saved X camera default to Q when Q is free; other custom keys and later explicit remaps back to X stay saved. R can be assigned elsewhere after rebinding Reload.

`CameraToggleConfig.CooldownSeconds` defaults to 0.5. ToogleCam uses native `Player.CameraMode.LockFirstPerson`, so zoom-out remains blocked until the saved key is pressed again. The lock survives unequip and respawn. Unlock restores Classic and the previous measured third-person distance, or `ReturnZoomDistance` (8 studs) when entering from manually zoomed first person, within current zoom limits. A temporary minimum zoom is restored after one native camera update; there is no permanent toggle render callback or character movement write. Chat, menus and settings editing block toggle input. VR and other camera subjects/controllers retain camera ownership.

The default budget is 64 weapon parts plus two arms and up to three invisible clothing shell parts. Models are built on demand, reused while equipped, and rebuilt when relevant descendants change. Missing limbs/Handle or transient failures retry automatically after 0.1 seconds, with error backoff capped at one second. Failed presentation restores the real weapon and releases listeners. Menus, VR and camera takeovers suspend the model; unequip, death, respawn and errors clear it.

Ordinary rendering scans no descendants and creates no Instances. Cached source part transforms are refreshed relative to the real Handle, so a multipart skin that finishes positioning after equip does not retain the clone's initial offset. Existing mesh offsets, texture face parenting and material settings are preserved. Property listeners follow later appearance changes; adding/removing textures or parts triggers one rebuild. Mesh-based arm slimming avoids applying the width/depth scale twice to block meshes.

Both camera weapons wait until the real `RightGrip` connects the current right arm to the equipped Handle. They read its current local `C0`/`C1` frame on every placement, including `Transform` for a Motor6D grip, instead of retaining an equip-time snapshot or guessing from `Tool.Grip`. A grip whose part references arrive late, whose offsets settle after equip, or which gets replaced cannot leave the camera copy rotated upward. Temporarily unbound grips suspend the copy and restore original visibility, then reuse the complete model when the correct connection returns. Grip replacement alone creates no new model, listeners or descendant scans. This follows the local joint frames described by [Weld](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Weld) and [Motor6D](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Motor6D).

Knife action tables and tuning vectors are reused. Support-arm offsets are cached at model construction. Visibility guards read cached faces and only write changed masks. Muzzle effects add no update connection, light, smoke simulation or independent moving part. Cached shot streaks add four inert parts in total, reuse the same active-tracer budget as observers and share one update callback. Each firing receives a small local lease so a delayed server correction or cleanup for an expired/reused slot cannot alter a newer shot. Recycling clears trails before teleporting the glint, preventing streaks between old and new positions. Suspension clears muzzle particles and stops pooled voices once; already-fired streaks finish their flight. Model replacement and character cleanup release the pool, including models removed after suspension. Native MicroProfiler labels `RevolverFirstPerson` and `KnifeFirstPerson` bracket presenter updates for measurement in the real place.

## Cosmetic muzzle and bullet marks

Edit `src/Modules/RevolverConfig.luau`, in `Config.Default.Effects`:

```luau
MuzzleVisualRight = 0,
MuzzleVisualUp = 0.25,       -- increase to raise flash and tracer start
MuzzleVisualForward = 0.45, -- increase to move them farther away
BulletHoleEnabled = true,
BulletHoleTextureId = "",  -- use "rbxassetid://YOUR_IMAGE_ID" for a custom image
BulletHoleSize = 0.38,      -- studs
BulletHoleLifetime = 8,
BulletHoleFadeSeconds = 1,
```

Positive up/forward values move the visual muzzle upward/away in camera space in first person, including skins with rotated authored sockets. World guns use muzzle-local axes. Both flash and tracer begin at the same adjusted position. These controls preserve the authored attachment and do not change the physical `MuzzleOffset`, aim ray, damage or wall checks. Use `Config.Overrides[skinName].Effects` for a particular skin, then restart Play after editing configuration.

The texture must be an image asset with a transparent background. Empty `BulletHoleTextureId` uses the built-in drawn puncture/cracks so the default needs no external image. A nonempty ID replaces that fallback and is queued through the existing background asset warmup. Verify the image's permissions in the published experience. Marks use server-confirmed impact position, normal and surface; prediction does not duplicate them. They follow moving parts, stay occluded by walls and fade before retirement. `Config.Presentation.MaxBulletHoles` bounds the pool; distance and surface offset controls live next to the texture settings. The mark update disconnects when idle and avoids rewriting unchanged transforms, visibility and opacity.

The stab is configured in `KnifeConfig.FirstPerson`: `StabSeconds` defaults to 0.42, and `StabWindupFraction`, `StabThrustFraction`, `StabHoldFraction` end preparation, extension and its hold. `StabWindupOffset` prepares the hand; `StabOffset` defaults to `{-0.34, 0.06, -1.35}`. More negative Z increases forward reach. Keep preparation/extension rotations similar to preserve an arm thrust instead of a wrist flick. The live character animation remains visible to observers and blends out only from the first-person camera hand during the stab. Rapid tap/hold inputs charge normally in gameplay; the camera finishes the stab before showing that charge. A newer throw retires the old stab, so the hand cannot combine competing poses.

## Weapon inspect, remote audio and death view

Press **F** with either weapon equipped and fully zoomed in to inspect its camera copy. Press F again to return early. Revolver and knife have separate side-to-side inspection poses, brief viewing holds and a smooth neutral return. `WeaponInspectConfig` contains the keyframes, durations, 0.5-second start cooldown, 0.14-second early return, equip grace and combat recovery. F is reserved by shared keybind validation, with no inspect settings row; a conflicting old saved F binding is repaired through the existing profile pipeline.

Inspect uses precompiled camera-local CFrame poses in the existing weapon render callback. It creates no Animator tracks, tweens, Instances, remote requests or additional frame connection. Inactive inspect performs no pose interpolation. It preserves normal posture/bob, source grip and skin geometry. Aim, reload, shooting, knife charge/stab/throw and damage take control immediately; sprint, slide, jumping or seating blend out quickly. Menus, chat focus, focus loss, unequip, death, respawn, VR, zoom-out and another camera owner cancel it. Start gates require a complete visible presenter; unsuccessful starts consume no cooldown, and a stale recoil call for an old Tool cannot cancel a replacement weapon's inspect.

Other players' accepted shots and knife throws play through `RemoteWeaponAudio`, independently of visual distance culling and shooter character streaming. Owners retain predicted audio once; observer VFX no longer plays a duplicate sound. `CombatAudioConfig.Observer` controls 24 reusable point-source voices, a bounded 256-event dedupe history, LinearSquare attenuation, and maximum ranges of 260 studs for revolvers and 160 for throws. Knife throws stay at full volume through 16 studs with 10% observer volume boost. Pools are created once at startup, preloaded asynchronously, and destroyed with their initializer. No per-shot Instances or idle frame update is added.

The death transition follows the exact server-created ragdoll through `DeathRagdollReference`, pulls back briefly and waits one second before switching to normal spectate or the remaining FFA killer view. `SpectateConfig` controls the duration, pullback and wall padding. One reused obstruction query checks the camera only during this active view, excluding characters/corpses/viewmodels and keeping it in front of collidable walls, following the [RaycastParams API](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/datatypes/RaycastParams). It leaves first-person lock/zoom and server respawn timing intact. Generation checks reject stale work on a new round, manual exit, game end or camera takeover. The server sends the dead Character with its KillCam message; the client rejects a previous-character message and briefly waits if Health replication is late.

Normal spectating hands the current live Humanoid to Roblox's native camera, retaining its tracking and collision behavior instead of assigning a camera transform every frame. A sorted, cached live roster reacts to membership, character and health events. Round kills, equipped knife, timer and alive count update immediately through their own signals; missing or unknown knife data clears the image. FFA respawn gaps keep a waiting view until a live target returns. BR automatic spectate intent survives the lobby respawn when delayed Health/membership replication overlaps the death view. Replacing Main or its spectate frame automatically rebinds widgets and the top button, with generation guards and cancellation for partial replacement trees. Owned listeners and button tweens are released on teardown. `SpectateConfig` also exposes bounded replication/handoff waits and button animation timing.

Knife equip now resolves the current Backpack and uses native `Humanoid:EquipTool`, with Handle/arm readiness, ownership validation and five bounded attempts. New accepted selections, another equipped tool or a respawn cancel old attempts. The hotbar follows actual character Tools and avoids duplicate equip feedback. Client inventory wiping no longer deletes authoritative Tool replicas after a later equip; server removal remains authoritative.

Roblox's [CFrame interpolation API](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/datatypes/CFrame#Lerp) and [render scheduling](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/RunService#BindToRenderStep) inform the cosmetic animation layer. [InputBegan](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/UserInputService#InputBegan) supplies the processed-input gate. [Positional sounds](https://create.roblox.com/docs/sound/objects) and [PreloadAsync](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/ContentProvider#PreloadAsync) guide sound distance fade and nonblocking asset preparation. [Scriptable camera ownership](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Camera) requires explicit Focus updates; [replication ordering](https://create.roblox.com/docs/scripting/attributes) explains why a death remote cannot assume Health has already arrived. [Humanoid:EquipTool](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Humanoid#EquipTool) and [Backpack lifecycle](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Backpack) inform the equip repair.

## Physics and camera ownership

Camera parts are anchored, noncolliding, nontouchable, nonqueryable, massless and shadowless. They belong to the server-registered `RevolverViewmodels` group, which cannot collide with any registered group. Clients wait for setup and registry replication before parenting a model. Cached guards repair changed flags. The invisible R6 clothing Humanoid is created fresh with state evaluation and movement disabled before parenting; no live Tool, Humanoid, joint, constraint, mover or script is cloned.

The real revolver and knife are also made noncolliding and massless, including late parts and skin-script resets. No player velocity clamping or root movement is involved. Physical muzzle/knife origin, damage, cover checks, ammo, charge timing and server authority stay in the combat code.

The posture camera removes its previous owned shift at Camera−1, native camera runs at Camera, ADS runs at Camera+1, posture lowers the view at Camera+2, and weapons place their models at Camera+3. It checks ownership before restoring so a replacement camera is not overwritten. Shifting CFrame and Focus together prevents accumulating height changes or corrupting the full-zoom gate.

## Research basis

Roblox's [render scheduling documentation](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/RunService#BindToRenderStep) and [task scheduler guidance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization/microprofiler/task-scheduler) inform the update order and limited render work. [Motor6D.Transform](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Motor6D#Transform) supplies actual animation posture; the revolver aim layer saves its animation sample before procedural aiming.

Roblox's [camera documentation](https://create.roblox.com/docs/workspace/camera) describes native first-person locking and zoom ranges. Its published [BaseCamera source](https://github.com/Roblox/avatar/blob/main/ReferenceBodyCreator/StarterPlayerScripts/PlayerModule/CameraModule/BaseCamera.lua) re-clamps the current subject distance when CameraMode or zoom limits change; simply selecting Classic retains the current close-up distance. The one-update minimum zoom adjustment makes the toggle's return to third person visible while restoring normal wheel zoom afterward.

Roblox documents [Texture](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Texture) as an image on its parent part face, and [DataModelMesh.Offset](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/DataModelMesh#Offset) as the mesh's local offset. The observed displaced image was not reproduced from an asset here; stale multipart positioning was a concrete renderer gap and is now corrected without recentering authored meshes.

[RaycastResult](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/datatypes/RaycastResult) supplies the accepted surface and normal for bullet marks. [SurfaceGui](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/SurfaceGui) lets the configurable image use a small plane aligned to that normal, including terrain and slopes, with native world occlusion. [CameraSubject](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Camera) and [attribute change signals](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Instance#GetAttributeChangedSignal) guide the event-driven spectator camera and stats. Published custom textures need the experience's [asset permissions](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/assets/privacy).

[LocalTransparencyModifier](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/BasePart#LocalTransparencyModifier) hides original body/weapon parts locally. Separate local modifiers for [particles](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/ParticleEmitter#LocalTransparencyModifier), [beams](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Beam#LocalTransparencyModifier) and [trails](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Trail#LocalTransparencyModifier) hide skin effects; hidden particle rate and light brightness are temporarily zero on this client. Unsupported legacy effects use Smoke opacity or enabled state. Already emitted legacy particles may remain until their engine lifetime ends when no local modifier is available.

[Collision filtering](https://create.roblox.com/docs/workspace/collisions#collision-filtering) provides a second contact barrier beyond part flags. [Humanoid.EvaluateStateMachine](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Humanoid#EvaluateStateMachine) disables the fresh clothing renderer's physics evaluation. This construction avoids references to the live character controller.

The [Decal API](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Decal#LocalTransparencyModifier), inherited by Texture, documents its separate local modifier. [ParticleEmitter.Emit](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/ParticleEmitter#Emit) supports bounded bursts; [Roblox performance guidance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization/improve) warns about transparent draw costs and changing emitter properties. Emitters are configured once and kept small/short. [CFrame composition](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/datatypes/CFrame) informs the camera-space charge translation. [MicroProfiler labels](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization/microprofiler/use-microprofiler) expose presenter update costs for device checks.

## Combat boundary

These changes add no remotes or new client-supplied hit data. Camera muzzle position, particle emission and charge pose are local presentation only. Existing server code validates revolver ownership/session/sequence, ammo/reload/cooldown, finite aiming data, camera/body bounds and cover before resolving damage. Knife handling limits request rate, validates finite inputs, bounds charge, checks equipped server-known tools, cooldown and physical origin/cover. Visual changes never authorize damage or send the camera clone's muzzle as the physical firing origin. Existing malformed-request and combat-authority regressions remain part of the test suite, following Roblox's [client-server boundary guidance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/scripting/security/client-server-boundary). This is validation of the touched presentation/combat boundary, not a security certification of the whole game.
