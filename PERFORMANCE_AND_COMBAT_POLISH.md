# Performance and combat polish

This pass targets concrete source costs and the reported equip, jump, inspect, slide, hit-feedback and knife-impact behavior. It preserves server damage, ownership, ammunition, kill credit and reward authority. These are source improvements with deterministic regression coverage; no before/after FPS or memory measurements have been taken in the live place.

## Equip and first-person motion

- `WeaponViewmodel` schedules camera geometry construction outside the render callback. `WeaponViewmodelGeometry` copies at most eight appearance objects before yielding, rather than building the entire rig in one render frame. The live weapon is hidden only after its complete camera copy is safely placed.
- Cancellation generations discard unfinished builds on weapon changes, death, removal and respawn. Late hierarchy changes restart a snapshot. Animated skin property changes are buffered and reconciled instead of repeatedly cancelling a build. Temporary build listeners and partially cloned hierarchies are disposed on every exit.
- Revolver muzzle particles, sound voices and streak slots are prepared with the deferred build. The existing bounded pools are reused during firing. Render-time tool searches now cache the empty equipped slot until character child events invalidate it.
- `WeaponAssetWarmup` requests only the selected knife/revolver, their relevant sounds and combat animations in background work. It serializes requests, deduplicates a bounded cache, walks the selected weapon in small batches and cleans up temporary typed Sound/Animation objects. Missing assets do not block equipping or camera/input handlers.
- Both weapons now use 0.012-stud walking bob, 0.9-degree mouse sway and less inherited body/arm animation. Airborne animation influence is 6%, its rotation is capped at two degrees, upward animation translation is capped at 0.035 stud, and air bob uses 6% influence. Recoil, reload and charge still have deliberate action motion.
- The camera support arm uses a continuous shortest-arc rotation in camera space instead of switching its up vector at a near-vertical threshold. Character shoulder yaw smoothly fades only inside a narrow steep-aim cone, preventing small cursor movements from swinging the arm left/right while preserving ordinary aiming.
- First-person knife stabs prepare the grip, drive the whole hand and blade forward, hold the extension briefly, then recover. The 0.42-second path has a 76 ms extended hold and a small consistent wrist tilt instead of a large flick. Inherited character animation blends out during the thrust so it cannot pull the camera hand away. Elapsed-time sampling preserves the movement at 30/60/144 FPS. Server damage, stab range and cooldown remain unchanged.
- Both weapons can inspect while sprinting. Inspection smoothly reduces inherited sprint pose, bob and sway. Aiming, reload, attacks, jumping, sliding, seating, damage, menus, camera takeover and death retain their cancellation behavior. Existing five-second inspect timing is retained.

Tuning: `RevolverConfig.Default.FirstPerson` and `KnifeConfig.FirstPerson` expose `BuildObjectsPerFrame`, `AirAnimationScale`, `AirMaxAnimationDegrees`, `AirBobScale`, `MaxUpwardAnimationOffset`, `InspectAnimationScale` and `InspectBobScale`.

Background asset budgets are in `WeaponAssetWarmupConfig`. Mesh/texture availability can still depend on Roblox content delivery and the authored skin.

## Combat feedback and knife impacts

- A lower-left dark kill card shows **KILLED**, the username, a headshot and **+N XP earned**. Fade, scale and stack movement use short client tweens. Thumbnail loading uses Roblox's thumbnail URI rather than a yielding avatar lookup during the kill callback.
- The immediate kill card and later reward reply share a server-created kill ID. XP is the value returned by the actual reward function, including VIP/pass and anti-farm effects; a suppressed award displays zero. Stale reward replies cannot update a recycled card.
- White open-corner hit confirmation replaces the font-based yellow X. Confirmed damage gets a larger, outlined 36-pixel floating number, independent of the overhead-nameplate template. Fixed pools bound cards, damage billboards and hit-sound voices.
- Every pooled damage BillboardGui explicitly sets `ResetOnSpawn = false`. These billboards are direct children of PlayerGui, alongside the persistent CombatFeedback ScreenGui; they need their own respawn setting to avoid retaining destroyed labels after the attacker respawns. Each confirmed hit attaches to the head of the character sent by the server, including respawned victims. Regression tests cover both respawn paths, 30 repeated respawns, pool reuse and stale timers.
- Thrown knives embed half their visible length along the inward surface normal. Walls, floors, ceilings and slopes share the same placement rule, with per-skin axis/tip overrides for unusual meshes. These anchored, nonqueryable visuals never affect damage or movement.
- Projectile visuals share the controller's existing Heartbeat instead of allocating a callback for each throw. Stopped projectiles stop updating. Particle-only impact anchors expire after 0.25 seconds; sounds retain their needed lifetime.
- Revolver flash and streaks use configurable cosmetic muzzle offsets, including skins with authored sockets. Defaults add 0.25 stud upward and 0.45 stud forward. First-person offsets use camera axes for predictable tuning even with rotated skin sockets; world guns use muzzle/Handle axes. A separate local attachment preserves the original socket. Physical firing origin, server cover checks and damage remain unchanged.
- Accepted environment hits leave a surface-aligned bullet mark on walls, floors, slopes and moving parts. `RevolverConfig.Default.Effects.BulletHoleTextureId` accepts a transparent image asset; an empty string uses a drawn puncture/cracks. At most 24 reusable marks live for eight seconds with a one-second fade. Marks are local, inert, wall-occluded and excluded from collision queries. Static marks avoid redundant transform and GUI property writes; the single update connection disconnects when all marks expire. Rejected shots, air misses and player hits create no marks.
- Other players' accepted knife throws use the existing 24-voice spatial sound pool, with full-volume distance increased to 16 studs and fade-out range to 160 studs, plus 10% observer volume. Integration tests forward actual server throw broadcasts into observer audio, including misses, wall hits, rejected requests and owner duplicate prevention.

Tuning: `CombatFeedbackConfig` controls card placement, colors, timing and pool sizes. `KnifeEffectsConfig` controls `StickFraction`, `StickDepth`, `BladeAxis`, `TipDistance` and skin-specific alignment. [Weapon configuration examples](FIRST_PERSON_REVOLVER.md#cosmetic-muzzle-and-bullet-marks) describe the new muzzle and bullet-mark controls.

## Sliding

The slide uses finite-force planar velocity aligned to walkable ground, smooth speed/steering and a short ground grace across seams. Small obstacles receive a bounded physical lift only after height and body/head clearance checks. Normal collision remains enabled; the controller never teleports through geometry. Real jumps, unsuitable humanoid states, death, menus and removal release the slide.

See [slide implementation and native checks](SLIDE_MOVEMENT.md). `MovementConfig` exposes the 30 Hz probe budget, step limit, slope limit, force, steering, grace and animation fade.

## Menu and memory cleanup

- The local overhead hides in first person with zoom hysteresis, using one short camera callback for the local player. Other overheads have zero light influence, brighter text and outlines on a fully transparent background while retaining wall occlusion. Their current world-space offset is 0.8 studs above the head. Character replacement removes the old overhead and visibility binding.
- Lobby lighting uses ambient/exposure floors to lift dark areas, with a gradual transition and the saved 20:00 night preference retained. Round and spectator views return to authored ambient/exposure values. The controller responds to settings, round and camera events; it creates no lights or idle frame update.

- Replacing an emote destroys its old animation track and disconnects its one owned movement listener. Delayed callbacks cannot attach to a replacement emote or respawned avatar. Preview tracks and locally created animation assets are destroyed on replacement/removal.
- Trade-row removal uses the same cleanup path for departure, a stale-row refresh and level restrictions, releasing its XP/trade listeners and cooldown entry.
- Case reels retain all 150 card positions, the server-selected prize at row 139 and the existing tween path. Lightweight cards are built in batches of ten. Emote preview worlds are excluded from those clones; a visible-preview pool reuses at most ten rigs/tracks, with at most two world clones and two track loads per refresh. Leaving or replacing the spinner cancels its callbacks, tweens and preview work.
- Spectating observes live character, health, round membership, timer, kill and equipment changes instead of polling UI or rewriting the camera every frame. The roster has deterministic player order, excludes dead humanoids and resumes after FFA respawn gaps. Missing knife data clears the old image; late or replaced stats reconnect. Camera subjects use native tracking/collision and release ownership to other camera controllers. Round end, respawn, manual exit and module teardown cancel pending death work and disconnect owned listeners.

## Research and design choices

The changes follow these primary Roblox sources:

- [Improve performance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization/improve): avoid expensive per-frame computation and redundant replication, spread setup across frames, bound transient instances and disconnect retained listeners. Camera-only visual copies stay local.
- [Task scheduler](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization/microprofiler/task-scheduler) and [RunService](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/RunService): keep render work short; prepare visuals outside rendering and update physics before simulation.
- [ContentProvider](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/ContentProvider): preloading yields, so asset requests belong in background work and should target the selected loadout rather than an entire catalog or world.
- [Raycasting](https://create.roblox.com/docs/workspace/raycasting): use the actual impact normal for embedding and query filters to exclude cosmetics.
- [LinearVelocity](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/LinearVelocity), [WorldRoot](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/WorldRoot) and [BasePart](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/BasePart): bounded planar force, swept clearance plus initial-overlap checks, and mass-scaled impulse for a physical step.
- [TweenService](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/TweenService), [BillboardGui](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/BillboardGui) and [thumbnail assets](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/assets): short client-side UI animations and direct avatar headshots.
- [CFrame](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/datatypes/CFrame) and [Motor6D](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Motor6D): camera-local rotations and interpolation retain authored grips and sample character animation before procedural aim. A shortest-arc camera-arm solve avoids a discontinuous choice of up vector.
- [Lighting](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Lighting) and [BillboardGui lighting](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/BillboardGui): ambient/exposure fill improves lobby readability; billboards can reduce lighting influence without rendering names through walls.
- [Positional sounds](https://create.roblox.com/docs/sound/objects) and [asset privacy](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/assets/privacy): accepted throws play from the server release point with distance falloff. The real ThrowKnife sound asset lives in the Studio place and needs published-game permission verification.
- [StarterGui respawn behavior](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/StarterGui): persistent LayerCollectors must disable `ResetOnSpawn` individually, including damage billboards parented directly to PlayerGui.
- [WorldModel](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/WorldModel), [Instance cloning](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Instance) and [Animator](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Animator): animated viewport worlds should be limited to needed previews; `Archivable` excludes heavy preview descendants from GUI clones; each animation load needs explicit track ownership and cleanup.
- [Debris](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Debris) and [ParticleEmitter](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/ParticleEmitter): retire transient effects after their required particle/audio lifetime.
- [Identify performance issues](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization/identify) and [MicroProfiler walkthrough](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization/microprofiler/use-microprofiler): validate frame pacing and memory on the actual client, including a lower-end device.
